The Challenge

A premium Lagos banana bread brand with zero paid advertising history, relying entirely on organic Instagram reach and word-of-mouth. Challenge: reach cold audiences systematically and generate consistent new customer inquiries via WhatsApp — with no website or checkout system in place.

My Role

  • Full campaign strategy, setup and management from scratch
  • Business Manager setup, ad account config, WhatsApp Business integration
  • Audience research, creative selection, ad copy development
  • Built a custom Google Sheets WhatsApp conversation tracker to capture full-funnel data Meta couldn't measure
  • Weekly optimization decisions, client communication and monthly reporting
Revenue Impact
₦33,000 generated
3 confirmed orders · ₦26,629 total ad spend · 1.24x ROAS in Month 1

Key Insights & Optimizations

  • Paid data revealed men 25–44 drove 72–77% of all conversations — contradicting the client's organic data showing a female-dominant audience
  • Static image (₦117 CPL) outperformed video creative (₦190 CPL) — challenges the common assumption that video always wins on Meta
  • Fixed location targeting serving ads outside Lagos — identified via custom tracker when non-Lagos numbers appeared; rebuilt targeting with Lagos pin-drops
  • Learning phase resets from early edits inflated CPL — enforced strict 7-day no-edit policy from Week 2, stabilizing delivery
  • Meta recorded 174 conversations; CSR received ~20–25 actual messages — diagnosed pre-filled message friction and simplified copy to reduce drop-off
  • Ads drove 35,700 Facebook page views (+223%) and 1,500 engagements (+100%) — paid campaigns built organic brand presence simultaneously

Campaign Details

  • Campaign 1: Static image · ₦10,749 spend · 92 conversations · ₦117 CPL
  • Campaign 2: Video + image · ₦15,555 spend · 82 conversations · ₦190 CPL
  • Total: 34,351 impressions · 1.56 frequency · 3 weeks

The Challenge

Test whether a small footwear brand could drive measurable customer actions — not just traffic — on a $3.36 total budget, with full ROI proof via GA4 conversion tracking. Conversion tracked via WhatsApp button clicks measuring genuine buyer intent.

What I Did

10-day multi-channel campaign across Search (70% budget) and Display (30% budget) with full GA4 setup. Made real-time data-driven optimizations: negative keywords, mobile bid adjustments, and audience refinement based on live performance data.

Key Insights & Optimizations

  • 88% of all clicks came from mobile — prioritized mobile-first creative and bidding
  • Search peak 10AM–2PM · Display peak 6PM+ — identified for future dayparting strategy
  • "Women slippers" hit 11.11% CTR · "fashion shoes" 7.04% — flagged for budget scaling
  • Male 25–44 showed strongest engagement — revealed gift buyers and household decision-makers
  • Negative keywords added: "free", "cheap", competitor names — reduced wasted spend
  • Search: 68 clicks · 2,380 impressions · $2.68  |  Display: 97 clicks · 1,210 impressions · $0.68
